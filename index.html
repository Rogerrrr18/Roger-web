03 / RESEARCH INTERESTS

Closing the loop between models and experiments

I want to build AI methods for materials and chemical research that improve through experimental feedback, with energy materials as a starting point.

01

Screen with uncertainty

Machine learning can approximate costly quantum chemistry and molecular dynamics calculations to narrow the experimental search. I want to study where those estimates fail on new materials and conditions.

02

Design candidates we can make

Inverse design should account for synthesis routes, available precursors, cost and stability alongside target performance. A promising prediction matters most when it can reach the bench.

03

Choose the next experiment

With limited time and budget, active learning and multi-objective optimization can guide the next batch of samples or reaction conditions. Failed experiments should inform the next decision too.

04

Make the research loop traceable

Link literature, simulations, protocols, characterization and negative results. Agents can retrieve evidence and suggest experiments; researchers and measured outcomes test those suggestions.

My test for these methods is whether results repeat in the lab and remain useful under realistic process or device conditions. I hope to develop them around a concrete materials system with a future research group.
